## Adversarial Self-Questions

- **What looks correct but might be wrong?**
  The fitted slope in Step 2 lands at ~2.07 pts/hour vs the designed +2.6. This gap is expected — the formula also includes attendance (`0.15 * attendance_pct`) and noise (`σ = 8`), both of which dilute the study-hours coefficient in the fitted line vs the true DGP parameter. The slope is still strong and positive, and the self-audit confirms the reported `r = 0.689` matches independent recomputation.

- **What would break if input changed?**
  The self-audit tests hard-code the expectation that all three claimed numbers match recomputed values. On a different dataset, the specific numbers would change but the audit pattern would still work — it tests internal consistency, not specific values.

- **What could a skeptic question?**
  The sleep null result (r = -0.022) is dataset-scoped. A skeptic could argue sleep matters in a larger or different population. The finding explicitly states this: "no measurable linear association in these 600 students" — not "sleep never matters."

- **What did we NOT do?**
  No non-linear relationship testing (e.g., polynomial fits, mutual information). Pearson r measures straight-line association; a curved relationship could hide behind a small r. The scatter plots shown are the visual sanity check, and this limitation is stated in the notebook.
